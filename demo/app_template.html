CorridorIQ

Wind-aware planning for urban drone corridors: routes, capacity and dispatch on real city maps and live weather.

How to cite

Ahmed, R. and Abdelghany, K. (2026). CorridorIQ: Wind-aware planning for urban drone corridors. Southern Methodist University.

Related papers: Ahmed and Abdelghany, Highways in the Sky: UAV Routing under Dynamic Wind Profiles in Low-Altitude Urban Airspace (Transportation Research Record, under review); Ahmed and Abdelghany, Shared Advanced Air Mobility Corridors: Configuration, Capacity, and Demand Management under a Reservation Protocol (TRB Annual Meeting, TRBAM-27-05211).

The city-scale 3D presentation was inspired by GeoWind2Plan (Qin, Zhao, Lyu, Zhou, Yao, Liu, Anandkumar, Wang and Xiao, 2026). CorridorIQ uses its own models and reuses no GeoWind2Plan code or data.
